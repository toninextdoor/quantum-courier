About Room 905

Room 905 is an independent multiplayer game set in a fictional hotel inspired by the Greater Toronto Area. Your crew has the hotel’s permission to recover abandoned property. The Sentinel is a robot. Contact with it resets your sensors and takes time off the clock.

The setting, characters and events are fictional. This game has no connection to any real hotel, artist, organization or challenge sponsor. I built it with ChatGPT Work and improved it through playtesting.

You can play without an account. If you create one, the game stores your username, avatar, optional bio and reviewed photo, password hash, sign-in sessions, XP, cosmetics and friend list. Your crew can see your name, avatar and room messages. Keep private information out of chat. The game uses screen effects and sound to warn you about nearby threats. You can change sound and graphics in Options. This notice does not mean the challenge organizers have approved the game.

      How to play

      Your job: You have the hotel’s permission to recover abandoned valuables. The Sentinel is a fictional robot. It resets your sensors and takes time off the clock. The game does not depict physical injury.

      Goal: Bring $600 worth of items to the trolley before time runs out.

      Navigator: Use the map in mission control to find EM faults, the Sentinel and valuables. Click the map to mark a route. Use quick commands or chat to guide your crew.

      Recovery: Move with WASD or the joystick. Look with your mouse, arrow keys or a swipe. Press E or GRAB near a valuable to pick it up. Tap E or GRIP at least every 2 seconds to hold it. If your grip runs out, you drop the item and it loses 8% condition. Press Q or DROP to put it down. Bring it to the green trolley pad to deliver it.

      Guardian: You can see the Sentinel, but you can’t see EM faults. Press F or SIGNAL PULSE to stop the robot for 3 seconds. You need to be within 6 metres with a clear path. Your pulse takes 12 seconds to recharge. The robot can’t move or reset anyone while paused. You can also collect items. Ask your Navigator where the faults are.

      Difficulty: Your host picks the difficulty. Easy gives you 4 minutes and a slower Sentinel. Sensor resets cost 3 seconds for EM faults or 7 seconds for the Sentinel. Normal gives you 3 minutes, with penalties of 5 and 10 seconds. Hard gives you 2.5 minutes and a faster Sentinel, with penalties of 7 and 12 seconds. Your goal stays at $600.

      Parkour: Each round changes the layout, item locations and Sentinel patrol. Press Space or JUMP to reach the platforms. Release the button before your next jump. The highest platform holds a $450 artifact. You can still reach the goal using items on the floor.

      Falls and damage: If you fall hard from an upper platform while carrying the artifact, it returns to its starting platform and loses 15 percentage points of condition. Dropping it from a height also returns it and takes 8 points off its condition. Sensor resets apply their usual damage penalties.

      Carrying items: You move slower while carrying an item. Hitting a wall while sprinting takes 12% off its condition. Dropping it takes 8%. Damage lowers its value.

      EM faults: Damaged power systems create EM Flux Faults. Only the Navigator sees where they are. Faults damage your suit and can reset your sensors. They also stop the Sentinel for a 4-second reboot. The robot must leave the fault before it can trigger the same one again. Your Navigator and Guardian can use this to stop it.

      Suit integrity: You start at 100%. An EM hit takes 50 points. Sentinel contact takes 100. After your first EM hit, you have 1.8 seconds to leave the fault before another hit. At zero, you see what caused the reset for 3.5 seconds, lose time and damage your cargo. You return to the trolley with full integrity and 3 seconds of protection.

      Sentinel warnings: Recovery players can’t see the Sentinel. The Navigator and Guardian can track it. Your screen edges turn red as it gets closer. Turn sound on to hear a heartbeat that speeds up nearby. The Sentinel can see you within 7.5 metres and hear you running within 10 metres. On Normal, a full EM reset costs 5 seconds and a Sentinel reset costs 10 seconds. Both return you to the trolley and drop your cargo.

      Your crew: You need 2 to 4 players. Choose your role before joining. You can switch to an open role in the lobby. There is one Navigator slot, one Guardian slot and two Recovery slots. You need a Navigator and at least one Recovery player to start. Guardian is optional. If someone leaves during a round, your crew returns to the lobby.

      ABOUT ROOM 905

      How the game works

      You and your crew recover valuables from a hotel suite. The Navigator sees hidden hazards on a map. Recovery players collect the items, and the Guardian can spot and pause the Sentinel. You need each other’s directions to finish the job.

      About the creator

      I came up with Room 905 and built it with ChatGPT Work. I tested it with friends, watched where they got stuck and used their feedback to improve the controls, rooms, graphics settings and cargo handling.

      The hotel is fictional and takes inspiration from the Greater Toronto Area. M.V.M is a fictional name created for this project. The game has no connection to any real hotel, artist or organization.

      Your profile

      Checking your account...

      An account saves your avatar, XP and cosmetic unlocks. You don’t need an email address or your real name. Your sign-in lasts up to 30 days and renews when you return in the same browser. Private browsing or cleared browser data can sign you out. The game stores a salted password hash, not your password. Save the recovery code you get when you register. If you already have an account, you can create a code in your profile with your current password. You need your password or recovery code to regain access. You can always play as a guest.

      Use your saved recovery code

      You can use your code once. Resetting your password signs you out on every device. Email recovery isn’t available.

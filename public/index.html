About this experience

Room 905 is an independent multiplayer game set in a fictional hotel inspired by the Greater Toronto Area. The hotel has authorized its fictional crew to recover abandoned property. The Sentinel is a fictional security automaton; encounters reset sensors and reduce the timer.

The game, its characters and events are fictional. No affiliation with any real hotel, artist, organization or challenge sponsor is claimed. Developed with ChatGPT Work and refined through playtesting.

Guest play needs no account. Optional accounts store your username, avatar, optional bio and reviewed photo, password hash, sign-in sessions, XP, cosmetic selections and friend relationships. Crew members see callsigns/avatars and room messages; avoid sharing private information in chat. Screens can show proximity effects and audio cues; sound and graphics can be adjusted in Options. These disclosures describe the project and do not represent approval by challenge organizers.

      Room 905 rules

      Authorized mission: the hotel has commissioned your crew to recover abandoned property. The Sentinel is a fictional security automaton: proximity causes a harmless sensor reset and timer penalty, never physical injury.

      Goal: extract $600 before the selected difficulty timer runs out.

      Navigator: use the 3D command-trailer hologram to see EM Flux Faults and distant cargo; click its floor to set waypoints, and use quick commands or crew chat.

      Recovery crew: WASD moves; mouse, left/right arrows or swipe turns your view. E / GRAB picks up nearby valuables. While carrying, tap E / GRIP at least every 2 seconds; if grip runs out, property drops and loses 8% condition. Q / DROP releases it deliberately. Carry them to the green pad to extract automatically.

      Randomized shifts / parkour: Every launch rearranges Room 905, its valuables and the Sentinel’s patrol route. Space / JUMP leaps onto platforms. Release and press again for each jump. The highest platform holds a $450 artifact; ordinary floor cargo is enough to win without parkour.

      Difficulty: the lobby creator selects Easy (4 minutes, slower Sentinel, gaps −3s / Sentinel −7s), Normal (3 minutes, standard Sentinel, −5s / −10s), or Hard (2½ minutes, faster Sentinel, −7s / −12s). Every mode has the same $600 goal.

      Guardian: first-person view reveals the Sentinel but hides EM Flux Faults. F / SIGNAL PULSE pauses the robot for 3 seconds, within 6m and a clear path. It takes 12 seconds to recharge. The robot cannot move or reset players while paused. The Guardian can also recover property and still needs the Navigator for gaps.

      Parkour cargo: a hard fall from the upper platforms while holding the premium artifact returns it to its original platform with 15 percentage points of condition lost. Step down the route safely. Dropping it from high up costs the existing 8 points and also returns it; sensor resets keep their existing damage penalties.

      Fragile cargo: you move slower when loaded. Sprinting against walls costs 12% condition; dropping costs 8%. Less condition means less money.

      EM Flux Faults: damaged power systems generate electromagnetic fields. Only the Navigator sees their locations. They reset player sensors and apply the selected difficulty penalty. The Sentinel is also affected: entering a fault forces a four-second reboot, during which it cannot move or reset anyone. It must leave the field before the same fault triggers again. The Navigator and Guardian can coordinate to lure it into a fault.

      Suit integrity: You start at 100%. EM hits deal 50 integrity; Sentinel contact deals 100. A first EM hit gives 1.8 seconds to clear the fault. Depleted integrity triggers a 3.5-second cause screen, cargo damage and the selected timer penalty. Respawn restores full integrity with three seconds of protection.

      Threats: A lethal EM Flux Fault costs 5 seconds on Normal. The Sentinel is invisible to your bodycam. Navigator and Guardian can track it. Bodycam edges turn increasingly red as it approaches; SOUND ON enables a heartbeat that speeds up nearby. It sees within 7.5m and hears running within 10m. A Sentinel signal interruption costs 10 seconds on Normal. Both return you to extraction and drop your cargo.

      Team: 2–4 players. Players choose roles before joining and can switch to an available role in the lobby. Slots: one Navigator, one Guardian and two Recovery. Launch requires a Navigator and at least one Recovery; Guardian is optional. If someone leaves during a shift, the crew returns to the lobby.

      BEHIND ROOM 905

      Built around trust.

      Room 905 is a cooperative hotel recovery game. One player sees the hidden dangers from mission command; everyone else must listen, explore and bring abandoned property back safely. Different views make communication part of the challenge.

      About the creator

      I designed the concept and guided its development with ChatGPT Work. Testing with friends shaped the randomized rooms, controls, graphics settings and cargo mechanics. This project is an experiment in turning an idea into a working multiplayer game through repeated playtesting and improvements.

      Room 905 is a fictional setting. Room 905 is set in a fictional lakeshore hotel inspired by the Greater Toronto Area. M.V.M is its fictional creative identity. This independent project has no affiliation with any real hotel, artist or organization.

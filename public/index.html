    How to play

    1. Assemble the crew. Join the same room on separate devices or tabs.
    2. Share what you see. Navigator sees red hazards; Spotter sees purple barriers, cyan parcels and green drop-offs.
    3. Deliver in 60 seconds. Driver/Pilot use arrows, WASD or touch. A collision costs 5 seconds and returns the parcel.

    With two players, the Spotter also drives. With four, both pilots share steering; opposing inputs cancel.

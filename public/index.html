    Ghost Shift rules

    Goal: extract $600 before the 3-minute timer runs out.

    Navigator: use the 3D command-trailer hologram to see invisible threats and distant cargo; click its floor to set waypoints, and use quick commands or crew chat.

    Scavenger: WASD moves; mouse, left/right arrows or swipe turns your view. E / GRAB picks up nearby valuables. Carry them to the green pad to extract automatically.

    Fragile cargo: you move slower when loaded. Sprinting against walls costs 12% condition; dropping costs 8%. Less condition means less money.

    Threats: invisible breaches cost 5 seconds. The Sentinel is invisible to your bodycam. Only the Navigator can track it. It sees within 7.5m and hears running within 10m. Getting caught costs 10 seconds. Both return you to extraction and drop your cargo.

    Team: 2–4 players. The first is Navigator; others are Scavengers. If someone leaves during a shift, the crew returns to the lobby.

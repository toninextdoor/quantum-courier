      Room 905 rules

      Authorized mission: the hotel has commissioned your crew to recover abandoned property. The Sentinel is an abstract signal beacon: proximity causes a harmless sensor reset and timer penalty, never physical injury.

      Goal: extract $600 before the 3-minute timer runs out.

      Navigator: use the 3D command-trailer hologram to see signal anomalies and distant cargo; click its floor to set waypoints, and use quick commands or crew chat.

      Recovery crew: WASD moves; mouse, left/right arrows or swipe turns your view. E / GRAB picks up nearby valuables. While carrying, tap E / GRIP at least every 2 seconds; if grip runs out, property drops and loses 8% condition. Q / DROP releases it deliberately. Carry them to the green pad to extract automatically.

      Randomized shifts / parkour: Every launch rearranges Room 905, its valuables and the Sentinel’s patrol route. Space / JUMP leaps onto platforms. Release and press again for each jump. The highest platform holds a $450 artifact; ordinary floor cargo is enough to win without parkour.

      Fragile cargo: you move slower when loaded. Sprinting against walls costs 12% condition; dropping costs 8%. Less condition means less money.

      Threats: signal gaps cost 5 seconds. The Sentinel is invisible to your bodycam. Only the Navigator can track it. Bodycam edges turn increasingly red as it approaches; SOUND ON enables a heartbeat that speeds up nearby. It sees within 7.5m and hears running within 10m. A Sentinel signal interruption costs 10 seconds. Both return you to extraction and drop your cargo.

      Team: 2–4 players. The first is Navigator; others are Scavengers. If someone leaves during a shift, the crew returns to the lobby.
